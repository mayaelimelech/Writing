Undying Moment

Maya Elimelech, Ephemeral Eternity, 2023

1.

I always knew music would lead me towards my death. Even in small ways, there were signs. For one, it always felt like someone was watching me. From the shapes in my closet, to the dark spots under my bed. This creature had no face, just shapes that I would try to fit together with my mind. There’s nothing really that romantic about it, I was obsessed with the idea of forever. My first taste of what that meant was when a friend of mine was bouncing on a trampoline with a hammer in her hand and fell, leaving a two-inch indent in her skull. Everyone crowded around her, stealing her air with dust swirling above them. I felt there was someone overseeing this, not causing anything but soaking in the permeance of whatever was happening. When I would fall asleep that night, I felt it kiss my forehead, maybe to take in something I knew or felt, though I could gather no motive. My mom once told me a story of a princess that existed outside of time, somewhere else where she was always the same and everything was ruled by ice. I spun in circles in my living room until I could feel it beside me.

I would get older, spinning in my living room turned into spinning on a dance floor. Deep bass and fast drums made me feel like I was turning and turning until the next day, like coins falling or something inevitable like a clock. I stopped seeing the creature in my closet as I got older, I only felt it sometimes.

2.

I’m in a club, it’s a hardcore night. I like gabber because it takes me halfway between heaven and earth. The sirens around me blast like angel’s trumpets and I submit myself to it. My friends had come up with me here to DJ in this abandoned building. I was told the owners don’t know the party is happening, and twice while they were setting up everyone had to hide as they showed up unannounced to play pool on a table upstairs.

“Take this.”
“What is it?”
“Mephedrone.”
“I’ve never done that.”
“It’s really nice, like a mixture of ecstasy and ketamine. Here take a line.”
“I’ve never done this before.”
“Oh it’s really chill, there’s no comedown really. I think it’s like plant fertilizer or something.”
“I’m scared I’m going to have a heart attack,” I yell in my friend’s ear, breaking the spiral of the conversation. “Aren’t you not supposed to do uppers and downers? I just did a line of Ket.”
“Its ok, I’m here with you,” my friend yells back, and I believe her. “I’ve done so much worse and I’ve been ok, and you would have collapsed in the first fifteen minutes if it would have done anything.”

Ania is the type of friend that feels like she was chained and locked in a basement during an important time in her development. What I mean by that is she sees the world how I did when I was 16, not now at 23. Still, she does a fuckton of drugs, and I trust her judgement. She takes my hand and we dance. I look to my left at the magenta glow of a light against the crumbling wall of the building. I see the dust pool and swirl there, like it’s alive, and trying to tell me something.
“I’m going to the smoking area,” Ania yells over the music.

I stand there taking in the music from my front, fully facing it. Ok just one more bump. I take the ketamine off my black and sharp acrylic nails, sinking deeper into the feeling of forever, all alone.

3.

Suddenly, the floor opens underneath me, it’s like a metallic thing with eyes, everything slow while it speaks. I sit down on a chair illuminated from all directions. The room has no walls, just black void. This creature starts to speak to me.

“What are you doing here? No, that’s not interesting. You know everyone here is going to die? The ice will melt from the ceiling, it will land on that girl’s face. You will all go home, and I will still be here.”
“Who are you?”
“I’m the one who exists underneath. I exist in the music that plays on and on, like a string independent of time.”
“Ok, why are you speaking to me then?”
“I need to tell you, because you are also going to die, what it means to exist outside of time. I want to offer you something. I want to offer to save you.”
“Save me?”
“You can live here with me, underneath the floor in the place where all the music goes. At the bottom of the whirlpool, the spiral. This is where all the feelings go, from each generation, to one place. They all form together into one moment, like the singularity of a black hole.”

Everything in this entity’s face is made from rectangles, everything smooth. Its skin is like glass and flows like liquid, somehow turning in a way that isn’t bound to any three-dimensional plane. I recall those images of the Seraphim from the bible. Be not afraid, they say to some guy. But I don’t feel afraid. Strangely, it makes sense that I’m in this room underneath the room, like this is what I was always edging on when I close my eyes. The ground starts to rattle, and I feel a grating tension in my chest, like my time there is nearing to a close.

‘You can stay here, underneath the dance floor, or in the heart of the music, if you like.”
“Why me?”
“Because you can, it could have been anyone.. and you are not the first.” The floor shook harder, and the void opened up to a long passageway with what looked like concrete walls. Its face contorted into an expression that seemed like agitation.
“Your challenge is to bring me something that can change me.
“Why don’t you just do it?”
“It’s an important task, what I’m asking of you. This might be your only opportunity. Now, go up to the top and bring me something, listen closely,”

Something soft enveloped me, like black goo. I tried to scream but when I inhaled, it felt nice going down. Like it was caressing me from the inside, like it loved me and was filling me with water that I needed. I didn’t know I was so thirsty.

I close my eyes and I am back on the dance floor. Feeling sick but still buzzing from the pleasure I had just experienced, I feel weirdly exposed. I need to leave the room. I can feel it still watching me.

4.

I don’t know why I ascribed this entity a gender but I wanted it to be a him. Angels are both genders often times, or none at all. But it felt like a him, in the way that I would never be able to understand it. I think about how reality is like a layered cake, with infinite possibilities for how life can manifest. Maybe it was some other type of creature that I shouldn’t trust, though I wanted so bad to trust it. Its words reverberate in my skull. I want to save you. Please, please be real.

I am still at the rave, just outside now in the ominous-ass entryway. The front entrance of the building has a little courtyard that the walls enveloped like something being absorbed and consumed. It probably was a factory of some sort 100 years ago, or maybe a school? Maybe a sweatshop where they whipped the children that they utilized for their tiny fingers, or a slaughterhouse with people who used to hate their jobs but now find it kinda mundane. I couldn’t really hone in on one image for this place, just a number of parallel realities for what it once was, living on top of each other. That’s what it always felt like in Los Angeles, hints of a history I had no business knowing about that the present loves to hide. Whatever it was, it isn’t being used for anything conventionally productive today. I find a little half destroyed ledge made of brick and sit by the weeds that are growing under the light of the sinking moon. The trees were shining so bright, the mist hung in the air. I could feel my skin loosening and my body slowly rotting. It’s cold out and my tights are soaked with sweat, but it feels nice.

I think of my mom, who was so different 10 years ago. The things she would say made so much sense; how do you cope with getting older and withering away, the water slowly being sucked from her skin. My grandma can’t even remember her own children, confusing street names for family members. I could feel my skin every waking moment, maybe just a few drops at a time, becoming more and more dehydrated. My mind more foggy like I’m forgetting what I came here for, thoughts evaporating. Is that why I go out? I am scared to die. Maybe I wouldn’t have to be, if I just listened.

Ugh this hurts my head. One of the two friends I came with walk up behind me with gravel crunching underneath her boots. I tell her I wanted to go home.

5.

Daily life for me is actually quite boring. I work at a bar to save up money because I’m not quite sure what I dreamt of when I was a child still exists (if it ever did). Every day I wrack up the same three orders, in a samsara-like rotation of life, death, and Guinness. It’s black, like liquid that flowed from the creature’s face and blended into the walls. I stare intently at the surface of the beer I’m pouring, like it’s alive, like it can shudder, like it has DNA of its own and I need to be careful not to spill it or it will reproduce in an uncontrollable way.

I think of him as I walk home through the park in the evening. I close my eyes and see its formless face staring back at me. There are men sleeping in the bushes, their eyes on me also. They feel like something I could fall into, like the absence of something that should be there. Daily life feels so erratic and temporary. I should be there also, on the string of melody where all the good feelings go. The flowers and the branches on the trees bend like I am underwater. Ok, snap out of it. I sit down on a nearby bench.

Just then, a tall, gangly creature emerges from behind a nearby tree, like a shadow sucking all the moisture from the air.

“I hope you haven’t forgotten about me.”
“You scared me.”
“But you’re still talking to me.”
“I just don’t know why you would assume I would want to leave my life.”
“There is no assumption.” I could hear a slight thumping coming from the dark of the trees.
“What is that?”
“It’s the clock of your life.”
“But everyone gets older.”

The beat grew louder.

“It can be so beautiful to live when life doesn’t have to end.” I shut my eyes and covered my ears, trying to drown out the music. When I opened them, he was gone.

If I lived forever like it said it did, everything would still wither around me. Coping with death on that magnitude brings to mind an interview I once watched of slaughterhouse employees describing day to day lives like everything was normal. I wonder if the creature could grieve anything. Could it love anymore? When death glides over your head because you’re under the water. I think love is just seeing yourself in others, I hope whatever it is that it can relate to me, that it can deceive, sometimes defying the plan set for it. The scarier notion is that it is perfect, that it can’t relate to me or anything alive because it’s already reached its unaltered state. I’m not sure how you would change something that’s already perfect, or why you would need to, if that’s the case then this task might be impossible.

6.

Am I forcing this? A picked flower, about to die. Something fresh and also temporary, that is something an undying moment would love. I wrap it in the pocket of my coat, and start home, looking over my shoulder as I did.

In my room, I sit on the floor with all the lights turned off. My room is sparse (I never felt like it was the right time to decorate it); nothing on the white walls and the fluorescent lighting leaves no room for shadows. Tonight though, the streetlight pours into the darkness in slits, as if cut up by tiny strings. I put on an ambient track, just droning noise and I close my eyes. I want to call him, to tell it that I found something for it. I picture its face, I picture the feeling of not existing. Melting, swirling like dust on top of water, dust at the bottom of a stream when the current picks up. Please let me in, please let me back in. I repeated this a mantra inside my head, clutching the flower in my left hand so it can’t breathe.

Suddenly, I am back in that room with no walls. A light shines on me from above in slits (something unfinished), and the ground swirls around my feet like oil in water. There he is, in front of me, his face huge like the moon. “I didn’t know if this would work, finding you this way…” It stares back at me. “I brought you this,” suddenly feeling childish holding up my little flower. I let go and the flower stays there, frozen in some sort of thick oil floating in space.
“I thought it was beautiful and temporary, and that that might change you.” My words echoed in the room with no walls.
“…Very good. I will take it and keep it here. Yet I am still unchanged.” Its face seemed to glitch momentarily with some emotion I couldn’t register, something about to snap. “I will give you another chance to be saved. I need something realer, and something that belongs to you. I need something deeper than beauty or pain …”

And the feeling is back. Starting as a suggestion from the tips of my toes, it crawls slowly up in a mass of infinitely small particles, sentient grains that slide up my legs then burrow into my chest. Euphoria as my body unfolds from the middle out, I hear only silence as I am sucked upwards into the light.

7.

I am back at a rave the next day, Saturday:

“Are you okay Rosalie, you look really fucked up. It was Ania. I know I have bags under my eyes, I didn’t sleep much after I came back to this world on the floor of my room. Now I can see his eyes everywhere, peering at me through the cracks of the walls, through the light that shone through my friends hair.
“Yeah, I’m fine,” I answered. But the truth is I’m impatient. I want to get back to him and that place of zero, of absolution. Down there, I felt everything at once instead of reality’s slow revelation of sequence.. Person… person… purple light… vape smoke… I’m so sick of it.
“Here take this.” I do a line of something the back of her cracked, black iPhone and it burns. The music feels like a pounding in my chest, I dance harder, the noise like tiny sirens pulling me under.

I have an idea of what I can give him today though. I see my target: a tall figure across the dancefloor in all black. I think that they look so fragile like a little cigarette that can bend and break if I just mistake it for food. I approach them and smile, knowing it doesn’t matter who I am, just that I could brush against their arm with mine. They smell like leather and blood from people fighting and scissors and cruelty, and all the cool things I can like while im young but stop being cool when I get older. Their eyes are black, something I can fall into and it’s fun. The walls are also black as they morph around us and we are halfway to the bottom of the ocean. I pull them close to me, closer than two people should be and I wait until I can’t feel my toes anymore.

The music stops with an echoing reverb, and it’s just us and void. My target doesn’t seem to notice, but from the corner of my eye I can see the 1000 eyes staring at us.
“I wish you knew how close this is to forever” the entity’s voice echoed around us. “You’re on the right track, but not quite. You still have your body. If you want to stay I need more.”

The music starts again like nothing happened and we float in little bubbles back to the waking world. They are still shoving their tongue down my throat when I push them off, to which they gave me a “what the fuck” expression. Yeah for real what the fuck, now down to option 3.

8.

I had brought my dad’s hunting knife to this party, something with many teeth he gave me three years ago for my birthday. I keep it in a box in my closet, where my shoes live too. I only take it out when I have friends over. With my thumb pressed against the tip, I find myself alone in the warehouse’s stairwell, decrepit lightbulb dangling above me. The lights on the dance floor were purple with fog shining through a crack in the door in front of me. This feels so uncivilized, everything was so beautiful. But it came to me in a revelation. Like a prophecy or an artistic whisper, I dig the knife into my forearm, slow at first then all at once, cutting loose a piece of my flesh, yum. I hear the music calling my name, “Rosalie… Rosalie.” I sink through the raw concrete of the warehouse floor slow like quicksand, cutting loose more and more chunks, pressing harder and harder with each motion and I feel what lions probably feel when they finally get to eat.

I am sat on that seat, this time with something to offer that was mine. In my open palm outstretched is the dripping meat I had just prepared, my forearm missing in jagged little a half moon chunks.

“Finally, something permanent! I can tell you really love the music, you are really the best listener, can you see the mark you’ve made will stay forever?” The creature laughed. The flesh grew and grew until I was staring back at myself. “I will keep this treasure here with me; and look I’m different now/.”

Its face morphed into something new, something that would scare me if I could feel any fear. The music became a beautiful cage, a kingdom of ice. I am deep underwater, far away from anything that could harm me because this was just my soul. The black liquid seemed to grow from the walls in waves, like drops with no gravity and the walls became my skin. I still trust it as it takes me in and makes me his. “It feels so good,” I think as the light from the ceiling closes away and I am left with just the distant pounding of the bass and this feeling of ecstasy, frozen inside me.

9.

The morning rose and my friends looked for me, eventually they stopped and the police stopped too, people mourned and moved on, people grew old and had children. They changed careers and cut their hair. I stayed the same within their minds and the minds of others who sacrificed themselves to the creature under the floor who gave us eternal life. I was happy when the music grew happy and sad when the music got dark; I lived in the place people only remember when they press their ear against the wall and wish they never left.
